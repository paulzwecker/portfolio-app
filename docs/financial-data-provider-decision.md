# Financial-data provider decision

Reviewed 2026-11-14 against official public documentation. This is a procurement
and coverage assessment, not entitlement verification or an activation approval.
No authenticated provider data was requested for this comparison. Exact symbol
lists and issuer evidence remain private; no portfolio identifiers were sent to
alternative providers. Prices and terms must be rechecked before purchase.

## Actual coverage requirement

The read-only production inventory has 22 held listings: 11 NASDAQ, seven NYSE,
two Amsterdam, one Copenhagen and one Xetra; 18 USD, three EUR and one DKK.
Security types are 20 common-stock listings, one ADR and one ETF. We need daily
unadjusted closes, separately identified adjustments/actions, and DKK/EUR plus
USD/EUR for 2025-11-10 through 2026-11-09, followed by bounded daily updates.
Exchange support is not proof of exact listing/share-class/ADR/ETF coverage.
Fundamentals attach to verified issuers; ETF holdings do not imply issuer statements.

EODHD is the lowest-priced full-venue candidate among the paid options reviewed,
not a guarantee of best quality or complete holdings coverage. Do not purchase,
create accounts, change providers or fetch paid samples without owner approval.
Google Finance pages and general-purpose scraping are not a substitute for a
documented, licensed machine-access feed.

## Smallest owner decisions

1. Name an existing encrypted off-provider destination, key custody and an isolated
   restore target; explicitly approve the current-state backup and rehearsal in
   the [recovery runbook](encrypted-production-recovery.md).
2. Supply existing Yahoo automation entitlement **or** approve evaluating EODHD
   Historian at $19.99/month, including personal-license eligibility and the exact
   24-subject API validation. No subscription has been purchased or enabled.
3. Supply a monitored SEC contact and review one private issuer evidence packet;
   approve only that exact mapping plus bounded facts/filings ingestion after recovery.

Keep FMP, Sheets, financial-model acceptance and unrelated jobs disabled. Do not
delay this first useful dataset for generic recovery frameworks, provider routing,
multi-provider consensus or a scheduler service redesign.
